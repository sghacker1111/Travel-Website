**Last Checked Date:** 2026-06-04
**Currency Exchange Reference Rate:** 1 USD = 133.50 NPR

## 2. Package Cost Verification Details

### 1. Everest Base Camp Trek (14 Days)
*   **Starting Cost:** USD 1,490 per person
*   **Verification:** Typical market rates for high-quality standard packages range from $1,200 to $2,800. The cost includes domestic return flights to Lukla (~$380-$400), accommodation, guide services, porters, Sagarmatha NP permit, and Khumbu entry fee.
*   **Sources:**
    *   [Nepal Guide Info - EBC Cost Guidelines](https://www.nepalguideinfo.com)
    *   [Nepal Tourism Board Official Permit Rules](https://ntb.gov.np)

### 2. Annapurna Base Camp Trek (11 Days)
*   **Starting Cost:** USD 850 per person
*   **Verification:** ABC packages average $800 to $1,300 depending on private jeep inclusions and Pokhara hotel tiers. Inclusions check: ACAP permit, TIMS card, local transport, meals, and teahouse accommodation.
*   **Sources:**
    *   [Third Rock Adventures - ABC Package Rates](https://www.thirdrockadventures.com)

### 3. Annapurna Circuit Trek (14 Days)
*   **Starting Cost:** USD 1,090 per person
*   **Verification:** Circuits range from $900 to $1,800. High pass treks require more guides and safety backup (oximeters, first aid). Includes ACAP permits and Besisahar/Dharapani ground jeep transport.
*   **Sources:**
    *   [Himalayan Trekkers - Annapurna Circuit Prices](https://www.himalayantrekkers.com)

### 4. Langtang Valley Trek (8 Days)
*   **Starting Cost:** USD 650 per person
*   **Verification:** Standard prices range from $560 to $1,000. Langtang is highly accessible by road from Kathmandu, keeping costs lower as domestic flights are not required.
*   **Sources:**
    *   [Nepal Hiking Team - Langtang Valley Trek Inclusions](https://www.nepalhikingteam.com)

### 5. Manaslu Circuit Trek (14 Days)
*   **Starting Cost:** USD 1,250 per person
*   **Verification:** Standard ranges from $1,100 to $2,800. Since it's a restricted area, solo trekking is prohibited; a minimum of two trekkers plus a registered guide is legally required to obtain the Manaslu RAP.
*   **Sources:**
    *   [Shikhar Adventure - Manaslu Fees & Permit Guide](https://www.shikharadventure.com)
    *   [Department of Immigration Nepal - Restricted Areas Permits](https://www.nepalimmigration.gov.np)

### 6. Mardi Himal Trek (7 Days)
*   **Starting Cost:** USD 590 per person
*   **Verification:** Prices range from $600 to $900 depending on Pokhara vehicle pickups. Short trail.

### 7. Ghorepani Poon Hill Trek (5 Days)
*   **Starting Cost:** USD 450 per person
*   **Verification:** Easy-moderate standard trek. Market price is stable at $450 - $700. Very high accessibility.

### 8. Upper Mustang Trek (17 Days)
*   **Starting Cost:** USD 2,390 per person
*   **Verification:** Market prices range from $2,100 to $3,500. Highly driven by the $500 Upper Mustang Restricted Area Permit and Jomsom domestic flight logistics.
*   **Sources:**
    *   [Hi Nepal Treks - Upper Mustang Expedition](https://www.hinepaltreks.com)

### 9. Everest Gokyo Lakes Trek (15 Days)
*   **Starting Cost:** USD 1,650 per person
*   **Verification:** Standard range $1,400 - $2,200. Slightly higher than the standard EBC trek due to longer duration and technical pass crossings.

### 10. Kanchenjunga Base Camp Trek (22 Days)
*   **Starting Cost:** USD 2,300 per person
*   **Verification:** Strenuous wilderness trek. Remote transport via Bhadrapur flight + long jeep drive. Range: $2,000 - $3,500.

### 11. Kathmandu Pokhara Chitwan Tour (8 Days)
*   **Starting Cost:** USD 790 per person
*   **Verification:** Market average for hotel/safari packages is $700 - $1,100. Includes hotel stays, Chitwan safari fees, Sarangkot sightseeing, and tourist bus transport.

### 12. Nepal Heritage Tour (5 Days)
*   **Starting Cost:** USD 450 per person
*   **Verification:** Ranges from $400 to $750. Covers monument entrance permits, private transport, and local sightseeing guides.
